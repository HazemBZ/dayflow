
Looking at your matrix and our previous discussions, your biggest risk is not lack of effort—it's **strategic dilution**.

Your system should optimize for:

1. Immigration opportunities
    
2. Career capital growth
    
3. Success in your new leadership responsibilities
    
4. Avoiding shiny-object overload
    

Everything else is secondary.

# The Core Rule

Think in horizons:

|Horizon|Focus|
|---|---|
|Daily|Execution|
|Weekly|Progress|
|Monthly|Career Capital|
|Quarterly|Major Outcomes|

---

# Daily System

## Every Workday

### Morning (15 min)

Review:

- Today's work deliverables
    
- Leadership responsibilities
    
- Immigration/job search tasks
    

Ask:

> "What are the 3 most important outcomes today?"

Maximum 3.

Example:

- Finish feature X
    
- Prepare team workshop
    
- Apply to 2 jobs
    

---

## Deep Work Block #1 (60–90 min)

Before work if possible.

Q2 Growth Activities:

Rotate:

- Terraform
    
- AWS
    
- Kubernetes
    
- Interview preparation
    

Example:

Monday → Terraform

Tuesday → AWS

Wednesday → Kubernetes

Thursday → Interview Prep

Friday → Leadership Learning

---

## During Work

Focus on:

### Q1

- Deliverables
    
- Leadership tasks
    

### Q3

Protect yourself.

When requests arrive ask:

> Is this actually important?
> 
> Can this wait?
> 
> Can somebody else do it?
> 
> Can it be documented once and reused?

---

## Evening (30–60 min)

Immigration/Career block.

Pick ONE:

- Job applications
    
- CV updates
    
- LinkedIn improvements
    
- Networking messages
    

Not all four.

---

# Weekly System

## Sunday Planning Session (60 minutes)

Review:

### Immigration

- Applications submitted
    
- Recruiters contacted
    
- Interview opportunities
    

### Career

- Terraform progress
    
- AWS progress
    
- Kubernetes progress
    

### Leadership

- Team issues
    
- Stakeholder issues
    
- Areas needing improvement
    

---

## Weekly Targets

### Immigration

Target:

- 5–10 quality applications
    
- 2 networking conversations
    

---

### LinkedIn

Target:

- 1 meaningful post  
    OR
    
- 3 meaningful comments
    

---

### Learning

Target:

- 5 hours minimum
    

Split:

- Terraform: 2h
    
- AWS: 2h
    
- Kubernetes: 1h
    

---

### Leadership

Target:

One improvement every week.

Examples:

- Better delegation
    
- Better meeting structure
    
- Better documentation
    
- Better stakeholder communication

# Monthly System

This is where major progress happens.

Every month ask:

## Immigration

Am I closer to:

- Canada
    
- Europe
    
- Gulf
    
- Remote international work
    

Which path is generating actual responses?

Double down there.

---

## Professional Growth

Complete ONE major milestone.

Examples:

Month 1:

Month 2:

- AWS Associate-level topics
    

Month 3:

- Kubernetes fundamentals
    

Month 4:

- Infrastructure showcase project
    

---

## Showcase Project

This belongs in Q2.

Not ten projects.

One.

Something that demonstrates:

- Python
    
- React
    
- AWS
    
- Terraform
    
- Kubernetes
    

All together.

A hiring manager should see:

> "This person can build and deploy production systems."

---

# Quarterly System

Every 90 days evaluate:

## Career

Can I now command a higher market value?

Examples:

- Stronger cloud skills?
    
- Better leadership experience?
    
- Better interview performance?
    

---

## Immigration

Am I receiving:

- Recruiter responses?
    
- Interviews?
    
- Sponsorship opportunities?
    

If not:

Change strategy.

Do not spend another quarter repeating the same approach.

# Time Allocation

A realistic weekly allocation:

|Area|Hours|
|---|---|
|Current Job + Leadership|40–45|
|Immigration & Applications|5|
|Terraform/AWS/Kubernetes|5|
|Interview Preparation|2|
|Networking|1|
|Showcase Project|2|
|Random AI Exploration|1–2|

Notice:

**AI experimentation gets the smallest budget.**

Not because it isn't interesting.

Because your highest ROI over the next 12–24 months is:

1. Immigration
    
2. Cloud/Infrastructure skills
    
3. Leadership experience
    
4. Marketability
    

Those four are likely to change your life trajectory. Most AI rabbit holes will not.
